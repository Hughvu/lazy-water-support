Privacy policy

Updated 15 October 2026

Publisher and contact

Lazy Water is published by Huynh Gia Man Vu. For support, privacy questions, account-deletion assistance or abuse reports, email huynhgiamanv@gmail.com.

Your water history and preferences

Lazy Water stores your water entries, daily target, garden, reminder settings, selected avatar, and optional name, gender and weight on your device. The app and its widgets share local storage so widget sips appear in the app. Exact water entries, weight, gender and daily targets are not sent to the social CloudKit database. Your device’s backup settings may include local app data in backups managed by Apple.

Optional social features

Social features use Sign in with Apple and Apple’s CloudKit service. The app stores the Apple user identifier in the device’s Keychain and local profile preferences. A hash of that identifier, a generated social user ID, display name, avatar identifier and friend code are stored in the CloudKit public database. A hash remains an identifier; it is not anonymous data.

Friend requests, responses, block records and predefined encouragements use the public database. Public-database permissions govern access, so these records should not be treated as private conversations. A friend code helps people find a profile; it is not a password. Do not use a sensitive personal name as your social display name.

This beta offers predefined encouragements. Custom message text from older or modified clients is not displayed by the current live service. You can block a friend from Personal or Friends to stop exchanging encouragements and progress. An offline block applies on this device first and is retried when you refresh; it may take a successful sync to reach your other devices.

Sharing daily progress

Sharing starts off. When enabled, the app sends the day’s percentage (capped at 100), day boundaries, update time and avatar, encrypted separately for each accepted friend. Exact amounts and your target are excluded. Sharing keys are stored in your private CloudKit database so your devices can use the same key. This is not a promise of end-to-end encryption against Apple or a CloudKit container administrator.

Progress expires in the app at the sender’s midnight. Turning sharing off marks it disabled and removes published shares when synchronization succeeds. Previously viewed data cannot be taken back from a recipient. Offline changes may require retrying after reconnecting. The app does not persist received progress snapshots to disk.

Notifications and other services

Reminders are scheduled on the device with your permission. You can turn them off in the app or iOS Settings. Lazy Water does not include advertising, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking in this version. CloudKit and Sign in with Apple are Apple services subject to Apple’s own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local history remains until you clear it, delete your account’s device data, or remove the app. Account deletion is initiated from Personal → Delete Account and requires authentication and a network connection. Successful deletion removes your discoverable profile, your sent encouragements, your owned connection/block records, your published progress, your private keys and receipts, and this device’s local history and garden. The app confirms completion only after Apple authorization revocation and cleanup succeed. If deletion is interrupted, retry the Delete Account flow. If deletion is unavailable or you need help with a request, email huynhgiamanv@gmail.com.

Records created and owned by other people, including their sent encouragements and requests, may remain in their CloudKit records. The current app excludes relationships whose profile is gone. Deleting your private sharing key prevents future access through that deleted account. Other people may retain information they already viewed, and device backups may retain earlier data under Apple’s backup policies.

You can use water tracking without creating a social account. You can withdraw progress sharing consent separately from account deletion. This policy must be updated if the app’s data handling changes.
